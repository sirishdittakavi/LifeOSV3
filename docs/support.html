LifeOS Support

LifeOS is a local-first planning, task, nutrition, weight, sport and progress application for iPhone.

Protect your data

Open My Profile → Manage Profiles → Backup & Restore to export a complete JSON backup. Store it privately. Restore merges records by stable ID.

Reminders

If reminders do not appear, open iPhone Settings → Notifications → LifeOS and allow notifications. Also confirm the Plan and Task remain active.

Before requesting help

Include your LifeOS version, iPhone model, iOS version, the screen involved and the exact steps that produced the problem. Do not attach a backup file unless you have removed personal, family and health information.

Contact

Open a support request in the LifeOS issue tracker.
