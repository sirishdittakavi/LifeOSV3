LifeOS Privacy Policy

Effective 10 August 2026

LifeOS V1 is a local-first iPhone application. It does not require an account and does not operate a LifeOS cloud service.

Information you enter

You may enter profiles, optional profile photos, plans, goals, tasks, schedules, completion history, notes, nutrition records, body-weight records, sport records and result check-ins. This information is stored in the app’s local container on your device.

Information we collect

LifeOS V1 does not upload your LifeOS records to us. It contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications and photos

If you enable reminders, iOS grants notification permission and LifeOS schedules notifications locally. If you choose a profile photo, Apple’s system photo picker gives the app access only to the item you select. LifeOS V1 does not request camera access.

Backups

You can export a JSON backup through the system file picker. Export is initiated by you and the file is not encrypted by LifeOS. You control where it is stored or shared. A backup may contain family and health-related information, so keep it in a private location.

Children and families

Optional child profiles are designed to be managed by a parent or guardian on the same device. V1 does not create child accounts, transmit child data to a LifeOS server, or provide remote monitoring.

Retention and deletion

Records remain on your device until you edit or remove them through available app controls, or delete the app and its local data. Copies you exported remain wherever you placed them and must be deleted there separately.

External pages

When you open the Privacy Policy or Support link, Safari or your selected browser connects to the website host. The host may process ordinary web request information under its own privacy terms.

Health disclaimer

LifeOS is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. Seek qualified professional guidance for medical, nutrition or training decisions.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud synchronization, AI processing or other off-device data handling, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before that functionality is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method on the LifeOS Support page.
